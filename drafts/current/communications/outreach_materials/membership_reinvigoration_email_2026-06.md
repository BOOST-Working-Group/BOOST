Dear BOOST Community Group members,

A short update, and a request.

Since the v3.4.2 release last August, BOOST has moved from specification work into use. An interagency effort funded through the California Board of Forestry's Joint Institute for Wood Products Innovation and resource conservation district partners is now underway, bringing state agencies — including the Air Resources Board, the Public Utilities Commission, CAL FIRE, and the Department of Conservation — around shared data requirements for biomass chain of custody. A validation tool prototyped against the BOOST schema is moving into real-world pathway testing for the Low Carbon Fuel Standard and BioRAM programs. And WWF-US is benchmarking BOOST against its Global Traceability Framework for Timber, mapping our entities to that framework's critical tracking events and key data elements.

What the standard needs now is breadth. The next phase of work depends on people who handle this data in practice: supply chain operators, certification auditors, agency program staff, software developers, and researchers.

**The request:** think of one or two people who should be at this table, and either invite them directly or reply with a suggestion. If it helps, here is a paragraph you can paste into an email:

> The BOOST Working Group (a W3C Community Group) is developing an open data standard for tracking forest biomass and wood products through the supply chain — one shared vocabulary that works across certification systems and regulatory programs, instead of a separate format for each. The standard is being tested with California state agencies and benchmarked against WWF's Global Traceability Framework for Timber. The group is looking for people who work with this data in practice: supply chain operators, certifiers, agency staff, and software developers. Membership is free and joining takes a few minutes: https://www.w3.org/community/boost-01/

Joining is free and open to anyone; substantive contributors sign the W3C Community Contributor License Agreement at sign-up. New participants can explore the data model through the interactive ERD Navigator (https://BOOST-Working-Group.github.io/BOOST/) and pick up a task or simply follow along on this list.

Thank you — the standard is only as good as the range of people who shape it.

Best regards,

Peter Tittmann
Chair, BOOST Working Group
peter@arbos.bio
