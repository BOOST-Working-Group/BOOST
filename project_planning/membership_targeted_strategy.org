* Purpose

Replace broadcast membership outreach with a named-seat strategy: identify the
specific people whose absence blocks the next two quarters of work, and recruit
each one to a concrete task through a warm path. Broadcast materials (blog
post, LinkedIn, CG announcement — June 2026 campaign in
=drafts/current/communications/outreach_materials/=) run as ambient air cover;
conversion happens through individual invitations.

Source analysis: the GTFT Milestone 1 landscape review and outreach matrices
(Arbos–WWF engagement), filtered for the CG-membership question — /would this
person actually touch the schema?/ The GTFT coordination list and the BOOST
membership list overlap but are not the same list; organizations engaged at the
framework level (e.g., EU TRACES NT) get crosswalks, not membership invitations.

* Prerequisite — charter amendment lands first

Nearly every target below is timber- or EUDR-scoped. Invitations sent before
the scope amendment (#283) is ratified will be read against a charter that says
"solid biomass for energy" — the invitee correctly concludes they're in the
wrong room. Sequence: ratify #283, then send the timber-side invitations.

* Protocol — one hat at a time

Most targets below will first be met through GTFT Milestone 2 outreach calls
conducted under the Arbos–WWF engagement. Discipline:

1. The M2 call is where the right person is /identified/. No CG recruitment on
   that call.
2. The CG invitation travels separately — from the BOOST chair, framed by a
   specific BOOST work item, on BOOST channels.
3. Never "WWF suggested I ask." The CG's multi-stakeholder independence is the
   asset; an open standard must not read as a single sponsor's framework
   wearing a W3C badge.

* Invitation template

Every invitation names three things: (a) the person, (b) the gap only they
fill, (c) a first contribution under two hours. Pattern:

#+begin_quote
We designed [entity/decision] without anyone who [their practice]. I'd like
ninety minutes of your skepticism on [specific artifact/issue]. Joining the
group is free and takes a few minutes: https://www.w3.org/community/boost-01/
#+end_quote

** Agency depth — convert attendance to seats

CPUC, CAL FIRE, DOC, and CalRecycle participate in the interagency working
group but hold no CG seats. Ask each agency to delegate one named technical
staffer as a CG participant. The ask is explicit and small: one person, GitHub
account, comment on the issues that touch their program.

* Routing through existing members

Nineteen members ≈ nineteen address books. Instead of "forward this to anyone"
(the broadcast version), bring this seat list to individual members and ask:
"which of these do you know well enough to introduce?" Obvious matches:

- CARB members → LCFS pathway holders, other-agency delegates
- Durrett (Green Diamond) → mill-side operations contacts
- Stäuble (PbN) → certification-scheme and EUDR-practice contacts
- Miller (Loamist) → facility pilots
- Carbon Direct members → biometrics/academic referrals

* Success metric

Count contributions, not subscriptions. Target for the September 2026 WG
meeting: /three new seats filled and working/ (a first contribution delivered —
issue comment, dataset reviewed, crosswalk drafted), plus the Wave 0
activations. Mailing-list growth is not the metric.

* Tracking

Work the table above as a checklist. Per-invitation status (contacted /
invited / joined / first contribution) can be tracked as issues in the private
BOOST Program project; this memo stays the strategy of record.

* Relationship to the June 2026 broadcast campaign

The blog post, LinkedIn post, and CG announcement proceed as drafted — they
make the room findable when a named invitation lands and the person goes
looking. The member-email version shifts from "forward this paragraph to
anyone" to the routed-introduction ask above.
