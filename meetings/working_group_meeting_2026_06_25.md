### 4. **Charter Scope Amendment — Presentation & Vote Opening** (25 minutes)
   *Primary agenda item. See PR #300 and issue #283.*
   - **Walk through the proposed amendment** (PR #300): scope extends from "solid biomass for energy applications" to forest-derived materials across three constituencies — woody biomass for energy; sawn timber and engineered wood products; forest fiber for pulp/paper/bio-based manufacturing.
   - Confirm the new **Out of Scope** exclusion (non-forest biomass feedstocks).
   - Note: deliverable title de-emphasizes "Biomass"; **no rename** in this amendment.
   - **Discussion / objections** — last opportunity to edit the text before the vote opens.
   - **Open the 30-day vote** per § Amendments to this Charter (2/3 of votes cast to pass). Establish the vote-recording issue; remind participants votes require written justification.
   - **Timeline:** vote closes ~July 25; ratified charter effective ~August 1.

### 5. **Rename Deliberation — Discussion Only** (12 minutes)
   *See issue #299 and `project_planning/rename_decision_framework.org`.*
   - Frame the brand-level question: does "BOOST" read as energy-only now that scope includes timber?
   - Decision space: Path A (keep BOOST, re-expand the acronym), Path B1 (sibling-under-umbrella descriptive name), Path B2 (framework-agnostic descriptive — working recommendation **OFDS, Open Forest Data Standard**), Path C (new pronounceable acronym).
   - This is **discussion, not a decision.** Rename runs parallel to and downstream of the scope amendment; no rename vote until #283 ratifies. Solicit stakeholders to consult and objections to surface.
